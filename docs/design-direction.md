# Editorial specimen direction

Mode: Read with an Experience layer. The maintainer explicitly requested unattended decisions and code-led implementation; the brief substitutes for an interview and direction-page approval. This is a chosen implementation assumption, not recorded human approval.

## Grounded directions

Ordered before implementation: (1) small-press review, (2) early personal-web directory, (3) urban field guide, (4) cultural quarterly, (5) library accession bulletin, (6) independent arts-journal contents spread, (7) pocket travel notebook. These span print periodicals, screen culture, reference systems and personal records. The seed assigned position 6. The predictable SaaS hero/cards and its oversized typographic-poster opposite are excluded.

The chosen arts-journal spread uses a bold custom sans masthead, a white reading ground, cocoa ink, a blue sidebar field and useful marginal lists. Historical orange and muted warm/cool colors belong in editorial art, not low-contrast body text. A reader at a laptop in daylight should see a clear paper-like page with strong color in its margins, not a faux antique object.

## Challenger judgments

- Exposed book binding: competitive on audience identification; its structural honesty strengthens visible content grouping, but decorative stitching adds no reading value.
- Daylight section: declined on both axes; keep its discipline of assigning each region a distinct purpose, without simulation or architectural costume.
- Data-sublime field: declined on both axes; raise density control so the sidebar earns space without visual noise; no flashing or data theater.
- Cutting-bench select rail: declined on both axes; keep an unmistakable current-page marker, without film controls.
- Civic prospectus: declined on both axes; raise coherence of all browser/control surfaces; no decorative ribbon or invented proof.
- Centre-rail reference setting: competitive on audience identification, weaker on immediate story discovery; retain clear separation of prose and supporting notes, without split article columns.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An independent journal whose lead story and useful margin prove a portable editorial layout immediately.

OWN-WORLD: Cocoa custom-sans masthead, white reading surface, blue sidebar, square editorial images, rules only where they separate functions. Flat, direct, no card stack.

STORY: Read the lead, discover three recent essays, use the sidebar's topic and reading lists, then follow an article into calm long-form prose.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact utility line and large left masthead over ordinary navigation; below it a 580:360 content/sidebar split with 20px gap. A linked multi-line feature title and blue geometric editorial image dominate the left; a blue publication note and contents list hold the right. Links are the primary action. At 36rem and below, a native hamburger disclosure reveals a full-width vertical navigation list; the sidebar follows the stories. The owner approved this phone refinement after rejecting the wrapped link row. Signature interaction: native in-article contents links identify the destination heading through `:target`, preserving scroll and browser history without JavaScript.
