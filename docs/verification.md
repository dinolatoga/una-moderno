## Layout, type and interaction coverage

Five surfaces run at **320, 390, 568, 667, 768, 1280, 1440 and 1566 CSS pixels**: the static index and article, Tailwind index, Astro index and Astro article. Heights are 320px at width 568, 375px at width 667, 1240px at width 1566, and 1000px otherwise. Static and Astro about pages use the same width matrix.

The implemented desktop tracks are exactly **1.618:1**, with `clamp(3rem, 4.1667vw, 5rem)` column spacing: 48–80px at a 16px root, approximately 60px at 1440 and 65.25px at 1566. At 56rem and below, a separate **40px row gap** separates main and sidebar without extra sidebar margin. Tests compare the rendered tracks and actual inter-column/stacked distances, not only declared CSS.

Display, navigation, section, article-title, cover-title and prose-subheading sizes are **80% of their preceding values**. Body, prose, metadata, decks, quotations and compact story titles retain their sizes. The wordmark uses two nowrap spans with a real separating space and `min(var(--una-text-brand), 16vw)`; prose enlargement remains independent. No utility row exists; tests confirm the masthead opens every shell with no reserved space. Titles use the full content width with natural wrapping, checked for one line exactly when their natural width fits. That holds at 320, 390, 612, 768, 1280 and 1566, at 100% and 200% text, without clipping or overflow. Inter remains the single optional local family; its provenance and fallback limits are recorded in [assets.md](assets.md#inter).

One horizontal navigation collection has 44px minimum-height targets, hidden scrollbar chrome, 24px gaps above 30rem and 16px gaps at 30rem and below. Tests check that the first label's ink aligns with the wordmark's ink within 1px at 4× sampling. That runs on seven routes at 320, 390, 430, 768, 1280 and 1566, with an unclipped focus outline. On phones, a scroll-timeline-driven paper fade marks the strip's right edge while destinations remain. Tests require it at the start of overflowing strips, its absence when nothing overflows, its clearing at the end and with the last link focused, and unchanged hit testing. Forced colors remove the fade. Forward and reverse keyboard traversal, immediate wheel movement after focus, missing fonts, 200% text, reduced motion and forced colors are covered. DevTools Protocol generates horizontal and vertical touch gestures with page JavaScript disabled; these are simulated browser gestures, not physical touchscreen observations.

Other regressions cover skip navigation, heading order, native article contents, local links and fragments, direct `file://` use, no client scripts, long titles, absent content images and root token overrides. Every link role is checked before and after hover on seven routes at 390 and 1280: navigation, wordmark, titles, recent stories, sidebar lists and text, prose, contents and footer. Line boxes, containing blocks and every geometry-affecting computed style must stay identical; only color may change. Screen, forced-color and print cases check the absence of decorative dividers and link underlines while retaining the 3px focus outline and 4px offset.

## Raster delivery, motion and performance scope

The five approved **1672×941 PNG masters total 9,117,372 bytes** and remain outside the consumer package. Ten WebP derivatives total **900,994 bytes**: one 1672×941 and one 832×468 file per scene. These are repository totals, not one page's transfer. [Master and derivative provenance](assets.md#optional-page-artwork) records hashes, approval, encoder settings and the limits of generated-art ownership claims.

The reproducible build uses pinned Sharp 0.35.5 at WebP quality 78. It verifies exact master hashes before encoding and applies only full-frame resizing/encoding. CSS selects the 832px derivative at 52rem and below, preserves the complete panorama with `background-size: 100% auto`, reserves 39vw before content and blends only the lower quarter into paper. There is no blanket opacity, cover crop or client randomization.

On every illustrated page whose body holds a direct `.una-shell`, the panorama is a fixed far plane. Where scroll timelines exist, it recedes at 0.4× the scroll through a CSS scroll timeline until the 39vw offset, under a full-width paper shell fade. Tests cover all 14 illustrated routes. They measure the painted art's shift between screenshots, require a root scroll timeline animating only `translate`, and check that no edge is exposed at five offsets. Reduced motion returns every route to a static page-coordinate composition. Without scroll timelines the art stays anchored, and `data-una-art-effect="static"` opts out. Print and forced colors remove the image, offset, fade and wash.

Raster regressions inspect selected-scene requests and response hashes across static, Tailwind and Astro routes; reject source PNG downloads and wrong variants; check the unillustrated chooser; and inspect all five derivative pairs and their near-white lower bands. Contrast sampling composites the actual raster at text positions. The full-cover wash is checked against the brightest washed pixel for paper text, light-rose hover and the destination-blue focus ring. This supplements axe, which alone does not certify text over images.

The delayed-image regressions block artwork until fonts settle, then compare geometry before and after image completion and require **zero attributable layout shift**. Lab measurements recorded at most 0.00092 total shift, from one font swap in masthead and navigation text that is identical on the pre-change build. This does not establish a field Core Web Vitals result; no measured LCP or production performance improvement is claimed.
