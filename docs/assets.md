# Asset provenance

No image, font, implementation code, or editorial content was copied from the historical Una ZIP or the private personal-site repository.

## Original demonstration art and writing

`examples/static-html/assets/*.svg` are original geometric vector compositions authored for this repository in November 2026, under the repository MIT license. They depict abstract windows, a skyline, a notebook, and window frames. These are deliberate flat color/vector fields, not historical screenshots or photographs. SVGs contain no script, external references, or embedded raster content. Copies used by the Astro example retain this provenance.

All specimen stories, dates, publication details and author names are fictional demonstration content authored for this project, under MIT. They are not factual reporting or endorsements.

## Display font

`packages/core/assets/bricolage-grotesque-latin.woff2` is the Latin variable-weight subset from `@fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque@5.3.0`, file `files/bricolage-grotesque-latin-wght-normal.woff2`. The upstream font is Bricolage Grotesque by Mathieu Triay and contributors: <https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage>. Fontsource distribution: <https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque>.

The font is licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1, not MIT. The unmodified license is included at `packages/core/assets/OFL.txt`. It was fetched from the public npm package and included without modification. No external font requests are made by the examples.

## Screenshots

Screenshots in `docs/images/` are local browser captures of this repository's fictional specimen, generated by the documented Playwright command. They are visual evidence of a local build, not production deployment evidence. Their exact capture commands and viewports are recorded in [the verification record](verification.md). Each PNG carries embedded origin metadata; [captures.json](images/captures.json) records provenance and SHA-256 checksums. All four shipping images were opened and visually validated.
