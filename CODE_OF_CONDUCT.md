# Code of conduct

Una Moderno welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces maintained for this repository.

Be direct about technical problems and respectful toward people. Explain criticism with evidence, accept corrections, give credit, and make room for questions. Disagreement is welcome; personal insults, harassment, discriminatory language, sexual attention, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions that cannot remain constructive, restrict participation, or exclude contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate this policy. Responses should reflect the conduct and its effect on participants. Maintainers are accountable to the same standards.

## Reporting concerns

Contact [the maintainer, Dino Latoga](https://github.com/dinolatoga), using a contact route the maintainer publicly provides. If no suitable private route is listed, open a neutral repository issue requesting a private conduct-reporting channel. Do not include sensitive details, identities of affected people, or evidence in that public request. GitHub's own reporting tools are also available for conduct occurring on GitHub.

This small project does not promise a response time or a staffed incident process. The maintainer should handle reports with care and limit disclosure to what is needed to address them.
