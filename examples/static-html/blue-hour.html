The city after the hurry

A walk home, taken slowly enough to notice what the afternoon leaves behind.

By Ivo ReyesOctober 9, 2026Field notes

Taking the long way

In this fictional city, the street behind the market is quiet for exactly twenty minutes. The deliveries have finished. The evening tables have not yet appeared. Someone sweeps a threshold into the light.

I take the longer route home because it gives the day an edge I can recognize. At the crossing, a blue wall changes color as the sun moves behind the buildings. Nothing announces the change. You have to be there.

What remains in view

Walking without headphones is not a cure for anything. It is simply another way of taking notes. A hinge, a conversation, the particular scrape of a chair: details that rarely make it into a photograph.

The city is still busy. My attention is the thing that has changed its pace. By the time the tables come out, I have almost enough material for a page.

Take your time.
