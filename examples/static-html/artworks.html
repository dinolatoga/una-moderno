choose a background

Each destination selects one approved panoramic background. The artwork fades into the paper ground, leaving the reading surface clear. These examples select their artwork in HTML; there is no client-side randomization.

  • Cat studioA curious cat, a working studio, and connected scenes across the skyline.
  • Robot cityRobots, buildings, bridges, and a city threaded together.
  • Ribbon worldColor ribbons wind through a panoramic world of towers, cables, and paper.
  • Kite festivalKites and paper planes above a broad, bustling skyline.
  • Desktop gardenA sprawling studio of computers, plants, books, and loose pages.

These backgrounds were generated for Una Moderno under Dino’s direction and approved for this specimen. No Microsoft or Zune assets are included. This chooser loads no background artwork; each destination loads one responsive WebP. The source PNGs and generation record are kept in the repository, outside the consumer package.
